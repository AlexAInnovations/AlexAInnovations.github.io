My approach

Simple systems. Clear handoffs.

  • Design: decide what should be automated and what should stay human.
  • Build: connect tools, data, AI, and business rules.
  • Test: handle errors, edge cases, and human approvals.
  • Improve: monitor the workflow and refine it as the business changes.
